## Overview

MEND is a student workload management concept designed around capacity rather than simply counting tasks.

## Core idea

The system compares a student's available daily capacity against task load and fixed commitments.

## Features

- Daily check-in
- Capacity assessment
- Timetable and task separation
- Task debt management
- Capacity-aware rescheduling
